# PROJECT_BRIEF.md

## Project

Project 1 final portfolio for AIM 5012: Effective Coding with AI.

## Audience

A course instructor, classmates, and a future reviewer who wants to understand:

- who the student is
- what the student has built so far
- how the student used AI responsibly
- how the student tested the final result

## Required sections

- Hero with name, role statement, headshot, and primary link.
- About section with learning focus.
- Projects section with at least three cards.
- AI workflow section showing instructions, skill, build, and browser check.
- Contact section with safe placeholder links.
- At least one simple interactive element, such as a native details/summary block.

## Success criteria

- Looks more polished than the Week 2 skeleton.
- Still uses beginner-readable HTML and CSS.
- Works on desktop and narrow mobile widths.
- Includes one small interaction that can be tested in the browser.
- Explains AI help in PROMPTS.md.
- Does not hide workflow evidence.
